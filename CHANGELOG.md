# Changelog

## 1.2.0
- New compendium "Arcano Loot & Luck: Zarabim e Mordisco" (D&D 5e, Adventure): one click imports Zarabim Trocafado, a mad gnome fortune-seller, his mimic partner Mordisco, their tokens, and a journal with his act, price list, lines and hooks. Texts in Portuguese.
- Install links in the manifest.

## 1.1.0
- Rewritten for Foundry VTT v14 (scene control button, DialogV2, namespaced audio, no jQuery).
- Arcano Lab look: purple and gold window, card back with the Arcano Lab mark, own icon.
- New reveal: cards drop from above and flip in 3D; rare cards charge up in their rarity colour before flipping, then burst into sparks, with light rays, foil shine and a stage flash for Legendary and Artifact.
- Spotlight reveal: Rare and better cards fly to the centre of the stage, grow, flip there and return.
- Cards look like cards: full-bleed item art and a frame per rarity.
- Recorded CC0 sound effects and background music (see `sounds/CREDITS.md`).
- A chosen player can flip the cards on their own screen; "Pick" mode lets only the flipped cards count.
- Optional cost per round, paid in gold by the playing character.
- Several decks with quick switching.
- History journal of draws and deliveries.
- Pity guarantee: after N rounds without a rarity, the next one guarantees it.
- Puppet mascot: a simple layered drawing with a talking lid, eyes that follow the pointer and dancing legs. He reacts to every reveal (anxious, unimpressed, happy, amazed), spits each card he deals, fidgets when idle and dozes off when left alone.
- Reveal sounds are now harp and music-box arpeggios with a long echo, longer for higher rarities.
- Cover screen with Nibbles the Mimic, the animated mascot (three painted poses and spoken lines): deck size, odds per rarity, table luck. He asks for 1d10 to decide how many cards, shuffles and deals. Players see the same cover when the GM opens the window.
- Luck dice window: strength slider, a live table of odds per dice result for the current deck, and guarantees (natural 20, or dice average reaching a value, guarantees a rarity).
- The GM reveals: cards wait face down until the GM clicks each one, or "Reveal all" runs the sequence. A setting brings back the automatic reveal.
- Full-screen fireworks for Legendary and Artifact (rings, golden willows, a rain of gold on Artifact). Can be turned off per user.
- Luck dice: the GM asks the players for 1d20; the average of the dice raises the odds of high rarities on the next draw. The window shows the new odds.
- Own sounds: card landing, flip, rising suspense, a reveal sting per rarity and a coin chime on delivery.
- The GM button lives in the Arcano Lab group of the scene controls when Arcano Lab Hub is active (token controls otherwise).
- "Give to" lists every player character sheet.
- Players watch the draw: the card window opens for everyone with the same animation (can be turned off).
- Give a revealed card straight to a character, or drag it onto a sheet. Click a card to open the item.
- Pool window: import a whole compendium by rarity, drop item folders, edit rarity, name filter, chance per rarity, default weights by rarity.
- Draw 1, 3, 5 or 10. "Reveal all" skips the animation.
- Options: no repeated items in a draw, rarity guarantee on draws of 5 or more, card sounds.
- Chat result with links to the items.
- Module language setting (follow Foundry, English, Português). Works with any system; rarity is read from D&D 5e items.
- Removed: the automatic macro, the button in the player list and the window opening by itself on load.
